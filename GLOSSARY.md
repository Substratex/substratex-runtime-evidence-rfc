# Glossary

**Runtime Evidence** — identified evidence material representing observable computational activity across runtime together with the source, method, scope, status, missingness, lineage, and preservation information required by the applicable RES profile.

**Qualified Source** — source material that has been identified and structurally evaluated for use under a declared canonicalization contract.

**Canonical Runtime** — a versioned, ordered representation of qualified computational activity with stable canonical identities and source relationships.

**Current Evidence Run (CER)** — the active evidence authority during evidence formation, binding source, canonical runtime, governing versions, formation state, evidence relationships, and missingness.

**Runtime Evidence Record (RER)** — a structured machine-readable snapshot of governed runtime evidence state.

**Certified Runtime Evidence Record (CRER)** — an RER issued under a declared RES issuance profile. Certification is scoped to declared conformance/integrity conditions and is not a truth claim.

**Bounded Finding** — a method-bound output whose evidence dependencies, support class, scope, status, limitations, and review state are explicit.

**Runtime Evidence Passport** — a portable identity, issuance, disclosure, scope, and non-certification envelope for an issued evidence record.

**Evidence Seal** — an integrity reference over a precisely declared evidence-bearing scope.

**Preservation Artifact** — a portable representation intended to retain or disclose the loss of evidence identity, lineage, status, missingness, review state, and integrity context beyond the active system.

**Support Class** — the relationship of evidence material to source and method: source-observed, computed, derived, proxy, interpretive, or experimental.

**Missingness** — an explicit state describing why expected or potentially relevant evidence is absent, unavailable, ineligible, non-computable, withheld, partial, or conflicting.

**Conformance** — satisfaction of a declared RES profile and version. Conformance is not truth, scientific validation, safety certification, legal sufficiency, or vendor certification.
