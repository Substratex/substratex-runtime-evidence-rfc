# Governance

## Stewardship

The Runtime Evidence Standard RFC track is originated by **Arjay Asadi** and published through **SubstrateX®**.

During the initial standards-track phase, the repository maintainer acts as specification editor and is responsible for versioning, editorial coherence, and maintaining the distinction between normative requirements and implementation-specific behavior.

## Design principles

Changes to RES are evaluated against six principles:

1. **Implementation independence** — conformance cannot depend on one product's internal topology.
2. **Evidence identity continuity** — evidence relationships must survive projection, export, challenge, and preservation.
3. **Explicit absence semantics** — missingness cannot be silently converted into evidence.
4. **Bounded findings** — a finding must disclose its basis, method, scope, support class, and limits.
5. **Non-destructive revision** — challenge and supersession add history; they do not erase prior states.
6. **Conformance is not truth** — schema validity, deterministic formation, issuance, or preservation do not establish factual truth, scientific validity, authenticity, legal authority, or safety.

## External review

External implementers may publish conformance reports without implying SubstrateX certification. A later certification program, if any, is outside the scope of this RFC governance document.
