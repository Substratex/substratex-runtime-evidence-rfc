# Mapping Note — RES, Content Provenance, and Verifiable Credentials

Signed manifests, content provenance records, credentials, and attestations may enter RES as qualified evidence material or integrity references under an explicit appraisal policy.

Cryptographic verification can establish properties about signatures, issuers, or artifact integrity within its declared scope. RES MUST NOT silently reinterpret cryptographic validity as factual truth, scientific validity, or claim sufficiency.
